# 90-Day Strategy

**Start date:** _set on day 1_
**Target:** $20,000 verified revenue by day 90.
**Constraints:** near-zero starting capital, $200/mo subscription, legal entity
exists, operator gives ~30 min/day.

## What the goal actually requires

$20,000 over 90 days is ~$222/day in revenue, or, by price point:

| Price per sale | Sales needed | Sales/day (avg) |
|---|---|---|
| $20 | 1,000 | ~11 |
| $50 | 400 | ~4.5 |
| $200 | 100 | ~1.1 |
| $500 | 40 | ~0.5 |
| $2,000 | 10 | ~0.1 |

**Implication:** higher price points need far less volume and are more
reachable in 90 days with no ad budget and no audience. A $20 product needs a
firehose of traffic we don't have yet; a $500-$2,000 offer needs a handful of
customers we can reach by hand. The market-analyst should weight opportunities
accordingly. This table is the reality check every plan gets measured against.

## Phases

**Phase 0 — Select (days 1-3).** market-analyst returns a validated shortlist;
operator picks. No building before this.

**Phase 1 — First dollar (days 4-21).** Build the smallest sellable v1. Stand
up the payment path (operator sets up bank + Stripe). Get to revenue #1 as fast
as possible — the first real customer teaches more than any research.

**Phase 2 — Repeatable sales (days 22-60).** Turn the one channel that worked
into a repeatable motion. Tighten the offer and copy from real feedback. This
is where most of the $20K should be earned.

**Phase 3 — Scale or pivot (days 61-90).** Double down on what compounds. If the
trajectory can't reach the goal, say so early and either pivot hard or prepare
the shutdown recommendation.

## Operating principles

- **First dollar beats big plans.** Optimize for the fastest honest path to
  revenue #1, then iterate from reality.
- **One channel at a time.** Don't spread thin.
- **Margin matters.** Prefer high-margin, no-inventory offers.
- **Kill fast.** A validated "no" in week 2 is a gift. Don't sink 90 days into
  a dead idea out of sunk cost.

## Weekly scorecard (finance-officer keeps current)

- Revenue this week / cumulative vs. goal line.
- Paying customers.
- The one metric that matters this phase.
- Spend vs. budget.
- Go / iterate / pivot / stop call for next week.
