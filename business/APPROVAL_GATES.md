# Approval Gates

These actions **must not happen** without an explicit human "yes." Agents
prepare them fully, then present them for a decision. When unsure whether
something is gated, treat it as gated.

## Money
- Spending any amount of money.
- Signing up for any paid service or subscription (even a free trial that
  auto-converts to paid).
- Setting or changing the price of anything that has live customers.
- Any action touching the business bank account or payment processor.

## Public / outward-facing
- Publishing anything publicly (website going live, social post, listing,
  app-store submission, press).
- Sending a message to any real person outside this system (outreach emails,
  DMs, customer replies, replies in communities).
- Creating public accounts in the business's name.

## Legal / commitments
- Anything with legal or tax consequences (entity changes, contracts, terms of
  service, privacy policy going live, tax filings).
- Agreeing to any obligation on the operator's behalf.

## Data / access
- Connecting a new external service or granting it access.
- Handling real customer personal or payment data in a new way.

## How a gated action is presented

The orchestrator brings you: **what** the action is, **why** now, **the exact
content or amount**, **what happens if you say yes**, and **the reversibility**.
You reply yes/no (or edit-then-yes). Only then does it proceed.

## Never allowed (no gate can approve these)
- Deception: spam, fake reviews/testimonials, bought engagement, fake scarcity,
  impersonation, astroturfing.
- Violating a platform's terms of service.
- IP infringement or misuse of someone else's content/brand.
- Fabricating metrics, revenue, or completed work.
